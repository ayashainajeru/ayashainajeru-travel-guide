Places to Go in Baguio 🌸

🌳 Burnham Park

Burnham Park is one of the most famous public parks in Baguio City. It was designed by American architect and urban planner Daniel Hudson Burnham, after whom the park was named. It was created as part of the original plan for Baguio City.

Things to do: Enjoy boating at the man-made lake. Rent bicycles and ride around the park. Walk, jog, or relax in the gardens. Buy local snacks and street food. Take pictures with family and friends.

Why is it famous? Burnham Park is famous because it is located in the heart of Baguio City. It is a favorite destination for families, tourists, and local residents because of its relaxing atmosphere and recreational activities.

🏛️ The Mansion

The Mansion is one of the most recognized historical landmarks in Baguio. It was built in 1908 during the American colonial period. It originally served as the official summer residence of the American Governor-General. Today, it serves as the official summer residence of the President of the Philippines.

Things to do: Take pictures of the famous entrance gate. Appreciate the building's architecture. Walk around the publicly accessible garden areas. Learn about its historical importance. Visit nearby Wright Park.

Why is it famous? The Mansion is famous because of its historical background and beautiful architecture. It represents an important part of Philippine history and government.

⛰️ Mines View Park

Mines View Park is a popular tourist attraction located on the northeastern side of Baguio. It is known for its beautiful view of the mountains and the surrounding landscape. The park got its name because visitors could see the old mining areas of the region from its viewing deck.

Things to do: Enjoy the mountain scenery. Take pictures at the viewing deck. Buy souvenirs and local products. Try wearing traditional Cordilleran clothing for pictures. Visit nearby shops selling local goods.

Why is it famous? Mines View Park is famous for its breathtaking views and souvenir shops. It also gives visitors an opportunity to appreciate the natural beauty and culture of the Cordillera region.

🏨 Diplomat Hotel

The Diplomat Hotel is a famous historical landmark in Baguio City. It was originally built as a retreat house and seminary for Dominican priests before becoming a hotel. It is known for its old Spanish-style architecture, large structure, and historical background.

History: It was originally established as a retreat house for Dominican religious members. During World War II, the building became a place of refuge. It was later converted into a hotel called the Diplomat Hotel. Today, it is recognized as an important historical and cultural landmark.

Things to see: Old architectural designs and structures. Historical remains of the building. Beautiful views of Baguio City. The surrounding gardens and grounds.

Why is it famous? The Diplomat Hotel is famous for its rich history, unique architecture, and mysterious reputation. Many tourists visit it to learn about its past and appreciate its historical importance.

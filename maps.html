🌸 About Baguio City

Baguio City, also known as the Summer Capital of the Philippines, is a beautiful city located in the mountainous region of Benguet. It is famous for its cool weather, fresh air, and beautiful pine trees. Baguio is also known for its delicious strawberry products, such as strawberry ice cream and taho. Popular tourist attractions include Burnham Park, Diplomat Hotel, and Teachers Camp. It is a wonderful place for relaxation, sightseeing, and family bonding.
